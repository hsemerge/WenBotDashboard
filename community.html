What are WenPoints?

The community's own currency, and it works everywhere. Channel points belong to one streamer. WenPoints belong to you, across every WenBot creator you watch.

Earn 1 WP per 5 minutes you're active in any WenBot creator's chat while they're live, plus 10 WP for your daily check-in above (streaks keep it climbing). Spend them in the community store on passport themes and flair, with more rewards coming.
