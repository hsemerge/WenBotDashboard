Every WenBot giveaway commits to a secret seed before entries open and publishes it afterwards, alongside the full entry list. This page recomputes the winner in your own browser — it does not ask us whether the draw was fair, it works it out from the published numbers. If the winner had been swapped, tickets added, or someone dropped from the pool, the maths below would not come out.

How this works

  1. When the giveaway opens, our server generates a random 32-byte seed and publishes only SHA-256(seed). Nobody — not the streamer, not us — can change the seed afterwards without that hash failing to match.
  2. Every entry is recorded with the number of tickets it holds. At draw time the whole list is hashed: SHA-256("name:tickets" per line).
  3. The winning ticket is HMAC-SHA256(seed, listHash:nonce), with the first 64 bits taken modulo the total ticket count.
  4. Afterwards the seed and the full list are published. That is what this page just recomputed.
  5. Every draw gets its own seed. The same moment a draw reveals its seed, the next seed is locked in and its fingerprint published — shown below as Next draw commitment. That matters for re-draws: once a seed is public, anyone knowing it could work out which entry list would produce which winner. A fresh commitment per draw means a re-draw is decided by a number nobody could see when the entry list was still changeable.
