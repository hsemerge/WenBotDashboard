What is this?

With two-factor on, signing in needs your password and a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds on your phone. Someone who steals your password still can't get in.

You'll need a free authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password or Bitwarden all work. It's optional, and you can turn it off again whenever you like.
