🛒 STORE

Spend your points on rewards. Sign in with Kick to buy, or use !buy in chat.

❓ How it works

Watch the stream, chat, and you earn points. Spend them in the store on real prizes, or on raffle tickets for the big-ticket items.

1. Get set up

Just chat in the Kick channel. There's no sign-up form, and you start earning straight away.

To spend points here on the website, hit Sign in with Kick at the top of the page. That links this site to the same account you chat with.

2. Earn points

  • 10 points per minute while you're active in chat. "Active" means you've said something real in the last few minutes. Messages under 5 characters, ALL-CAPS shouting and !commands don't count.
  • Subscribers earn double, so 20 per minute.
  • +25 points the first time you follow the channel.
  • Daily check-in bonus, given automatically for your first real message each day. Nothing to type. It starts at 50 points and grows with consecutive days: day 2 pays 100, day 3 pays 150, up to 350 a day once you've shown up 7 days running. Miss a day and the streak restarts.

3. Check your balance

Type !points in Kick chat, or sign in here and your balance shows at the top of every page.

4. How raffles work

Raffles are right here in the store. They're the cards marked 🎟 RAFFLE ENTRY. Everything else is a straight purchase.

  • Each ticket costs points, and you can buy as many as you like, using the +/- stepper on the card. More tickets, better odds.
  • Your ticket count and the total entries show on the card once you're signed in.
  • When the raffle closes, Meg draws the winner live on stream and announces it in chat and Discord. Winners also appear under Past Winners.

Some prizes appear twice on purpose: once as a cheap raffle ticket, and once at full price as a direct buy. The raffle is the gamble; the direct buy is guaranteed if you save up.

Stuck on something? Ask in chat or in the Discord and Meg or a mod will sort you out.
